Privacy Policy for Radio TV KX+

Effective Date: June 5, 2026
Last Updated: June 5, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Radio TV KX+ ("we", "our", or "the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application. Radio TV KX+ is a free media streaming application that provides access to global radio stations and TV channels.

By using Radio TV KX+, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please discontinue use of the App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

Radio TV KX+ does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use the App. However, we may collect:

  • Favorites and Preferences: Channel selections, favorite stations, and playback preferences are stored locally on your device only to enhance your user experience.
  • Local Files: If you choose to play local audio files (MP3, M4A, FLAC, etc.), these files remain on your device and are not uploaded, transmitted, or accessed by us.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you use Radio TV KX+, certain technical information may be collected automatically:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, mobile network information.
  • Usage Data: App features accessed, channels played, playback duration, interaction with in-app elements, and crash reports.
  • Network Information: The App detects your network connection status (Wi-Fi, mobile data) to optimize streaming quality.
  • Country/Region Detection: We may infer your approximate geographic location based on IP address or network information to provide region-relevant content and channel recommendations.

1.3 Information from Third-Party Sources

Radio TV KX+ streams content from publicly available sources:

  • Radio Stations: Streams are sourced from the Radio Browser API and other public radio directories.
  • TV Channels: TV streams are sourced from the IPTV-ORG GitHub repository and other publicly available M3U playlists.
  • YouTube Live Channels: Some TV channels may stream via YouTube's embedded player. YouTube's own Privacy Policy applies to these streams.

We do not control these third-party services and are not responsible for their privacy practices.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected for the following purposes:

  • App Functionality: To provide access to radio and TV streams, manage playback controls, and enable favorites.
  • Personalization: To remember your favorite channels, last played station, and preferences (stored locally on your device).
  • Performance Optimization: To improve app performance, fix bugs, and analyze usage patterns.
  • Content Discovery: To suggest channels based on your country, language, or usage patterns.
  • Network Management: To optimize streaming quality based on your network connection (Wi-Fi vs. mobile data).

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Local Storage

All user data, including favorites, playback history, and preferences, is stored locally on your device using:

  • Local Storage API (for web-based app components)
  • File System API (for local file access with user permission)
  • Capacitor Storage Plugin (for native Android data storage)

This data is never transmitted to external servers unless explicitly stated (e.g., when streaming from third-party sources).

3.2 Security Measures

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information:

  • No Cloud Storage: User data is not uploaded to cloud servers.
  • HTTPS Streaming: All radio and TV streams use secure HTTPS connections where available.
  • Permission-Based Access: The App only requests device permissions necessary for its functionality (e.g., network access, local file access).

However, please be aware that no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

4. Third-Party Services

Radio TV KX+ integrates with the following third-party services:

4.1 Radio Browser API

  • Purpose: Provides access to a global database of radio stations.
  • Data Shared: Your IP address and device information may be visible to Radio Browser servers when streaming.
  • Privacy Policy: Radio Browser Privacy

4.2 IPTV-ORG

  • Purpose: Provides publicly available TV channel streams.
  • Data Shared: Your IP address may be visible to stream providers when accessing TV channels.
  • Source: IPTV-ORG GitHub

4.3 YouTube

  • Purpose: Some TV channels stream via YouTube's embedded player.
  • Data Shared: YouTube may collect data according to its own privacy policy.
  • Privacy Policy: YouTube Privacy Policy

4.4 HLS.js

  • Purpose: Open-source library used for streaming HLS (HTTP Live Streaming) video content.
  • Data Shared: None. HLS.js operates locally on your device.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

Information may be disclosed in the following limited circumstances:

  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, court order, or government regulation.
  • Protection of Rights: To protect the rights, property, or safety of Radio TV KX+, our users, or the public.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user data may be transferred as part of the transaction.

6. Children's Privacy

Radio TV KX+ is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us, and we will take steps to delete such information.

7. Your Rights and Choices

7.1 Access and Control

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control:

  • Delete Data: You can clear app data or uninstall the App to delete all stored information.
  • Manage Favorites: You can add or remove favorite channels at any time within the App.
  • Permissions: You can manage app permissions (e.g., network access, storage access) through your device settings.

7.2 Opt-Out of Data Collection

  • Usage Analytics: Currently, the App does not use third-party analytics tools. If this changes, we will update this policy and provide opt-out options.

8. International Users

Radio TV KX+ is available globally. If you use the App from outside your country of residence, please be aware that your information may be transferred to and processed in countries with different data protection laws.

Your continued use of Radio TV KX+ after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
